What if the page needs JavaScript, or is behind Cloudflare?

We render pages with a real browser engine, so JavaScript-heavy pricing pages (SPAs, React apps) work fine. For aggressively protected sites — Cloudflare, DataDome — we try, and if the page won't load, we pause that monitor and tell you, rather than hammering the site and getting your IP banned. If pricing is behind a login, we can't see it; most pricing pages are public.
