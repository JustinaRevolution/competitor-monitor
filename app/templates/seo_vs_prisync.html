PriceGazer vs Prisync: you don't need the machinery

Prisync is built for teams: dynamic repricing, channel integrations, and pricing to match. Plans start at $99/month and run to several hundred. If you're one operator tracking 40–100 competitor SKUs, you're paying for machinery you'll never open. PriceGazer does the one thing you actually need at that scale — an accurate email when a competitor's price changes — for $19.

Why people leave Prisync

Prisync is a genuinely capable platform — if you have a pricing team to run it. Dynamic repricing rules, marketplace and channel integrations, automated price-matching: that's real infrastructure, and it costs real money to build and maintain. For a business with dedicated pricing headcount, the $99+/month is a fair trade.

But most of that machinery sits unused if you're a solo operator, a small e-commerce team, or a founder who just wants to know when a competitor moves. You end up configuring a repricing engine to answer a question that's really just: "did they change the price, yes or no."

How PriceGazer is different

PriceGazer skips the repricing engine and the channel integrations entirely. It does one job: watch the competitor URLs you give it, and email you when the price actually changes. No configuration beyond pasting in URLs, no dashboard full of settings you'll never touch.

That focus is what keeps it at $19/month instead of $99+. You're not subsidizing features built for a pricing team you don't have.
